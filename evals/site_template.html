03 / How it works

Three steps, inside one skill.

Most writing skills are a list of patterns to avoid. Brogue also checks the draft against the request, because invented details read as well as true ones and no pattern list catches them.

Find the reader

Who reads it, what they do next, how they expect to be addressed and how they will use the page. A senior client gets a request; an engineer gets commands in code blocks.

Write from what you gave

The request is treated as everything known about your situation. Everything you supplied goes in, with the conclusion it adds up to. Padding toward the usual length is where invention comes from, so there is none.

Strike what you didn't

Each sentence is checked against the request. Anything it cannot point to is cut. With a shell, the agent runs scripts/ground.py as a second model call that refuses to add or drop a number.

04 / Limits

What the numbers do not say.

Full method, scripts and every recorded output are in the evals folder. One command reruns it.

  • One writer model, . Other models may rank differently.
  • The auditor and the readers are models from the same family as the writer, not people.
  • Brogue's texts are shorter. Fewer words leave less room for invention, and that is part of how it works, not an accident of measurement.
  • Nothing reaches zero. Read anything that makes a claim about your product before you publish it.
  • English only. No claim about AI detectors.
  • The tasks were generated by a model from job roles in agency-agents, not written by the author, and the reported set was run once, after the skill was frozen.
