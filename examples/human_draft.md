We shipped the new onboarding flow on March 3. Setup used to take about 14 minutes; it now takes 6.

Most of that came from two changes. We stopped asking for billing details before the first project, and we import settings from GitHub instead of making people retype them. Priya's team did the import work, and it was fiddlier than it sounds because of how org permissions are scoped.

Activation was up 22% in March. I don't know yet how much of that is the redesign and how much is the conference traffic, so I'd wait for April before putting it in a deck.

One thing still bothers me. The skip button on step three is easy to miss on mobile. We have a fix in review.
